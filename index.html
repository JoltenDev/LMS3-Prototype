    LDWS warnings cannot be switched off while LMS is on.

    Keyboard: W or ↑ faster, S or ↓ slower, A or ← steer left, D or → steer right (hold), H hands on or off wheel, Enter acknowledge warning.

    Subsystems

    Acknowledging silences the chime and stops the flashing. It does not clear a fault or re-engage LMS. Every LMS disengagement raises a warning until acknowledged. Keyboard shortcut: Enter.

    Assumptions: override threshold is 3 Nm at 50 mph and scales with speed (4 Nm in bad road conditions). Required path certainty is 40% at 35 mph, plus 10% per 10 mph.
